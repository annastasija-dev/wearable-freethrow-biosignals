Free-throw data collection
VILNIUS TECH · Department of Electronic Systems

Install
1. Install FT-Protocol.apk on the phone.
2. FT Watch is installed from the phone app: FT Protocol → ⋮ → Install on watch. The phone and the watch must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. Open FT Watch on the watch and leave it open.

Sensors (once on the watch)
Settings → Apps → Health Sensor Service → tap the name about 10 times → Developer mode ON.

Before each session
Open FT Watch. On the phone tap Test cloud connection (must be OK).

Session
1. Watch on the wrist.
2. Enter participant code, weight, height, age, sex, throwing technique.
3. Start session.
4. 10 free throws; after each shot tap Hit or Miss.
5. Finish session.

Recorded signals
Accelerometer (x, y, z), heart rate (bpm), and PPG (green, IR, red). If the watch is worn for longer and the participant is at rest, skin temperature is also recorded.

After Finish session the record appears in the results folder.
